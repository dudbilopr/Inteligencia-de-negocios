English as a Medium of Instruction: Final Pitch & Data Storytelling

Analytical Context

A successful final pitch bridges the gap between complex data architecture and real-world business impact. Your dashboard is the supporting actor; you are the storyteller.

During a pitch, rely on your pre-calculated KPIs, leverage your interactive filters to answer questions on the fly, and always tie your findings back to a quantifiable ROI (Return on Investment). The ultimate test of a BI project is not its code complexity, but whether it incites action.
