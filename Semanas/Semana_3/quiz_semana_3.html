English as a Medium of Instruction: DAX Fundamentals: Evaluation Context

Analytical Context

Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) relies heavily on two types of evaluation contexts: Filter Context and Row Context.

Filter context is applied by the visual elements (like slicers, rows in a matrix, or page filters) before the calculation is performed. Row context, on the other hand, is the concept of 'current row' and exists inside calculated columns or iterative functions (like SUMX, AVERAGEX). Understanding how CALCULATE() transitions row context into filter context is the most critical concept in DAX mastery.
