English as a Medium of Instruction: Principles of Data Visualization

Analytical Context

When selecting visual representations, the goal is to reduce cognitive load and facilitate rapid pattern recognition. The human brain struggles to accurately compare angles and areas, making pie charts and 3D charts notoriously poor choices for comparing similar values.

Instead, bar charts (for categorical comparisons) and line charts (for continuous time series) leverage our brain's highly accurate ability to judge length and position along a common baseline.
