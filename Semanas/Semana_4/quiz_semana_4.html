English as a Medium of Instruction: Time Intelligence & Date Dimensions

Analytical Context

Time Intelligence in BI allows for comparative analysis over time periods (Year-Over-Year, Year-To-Date, etc.).

For these functions to work correctly in DAX, a standard Date Table (Calendar Table) is strictly required. This table must contain a continuous, unbroken sequence of dates, cover all years present in the fact table, and be marked as a 'Date Table' in the model. Relying on auto-generated datetime hierarchies creates hidden tables that bloat the memory model and reduce performance.
