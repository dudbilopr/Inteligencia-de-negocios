English as a Medium of Instruction: Data Model Optimization & VertiPaq

Analytical Context

The performance of a BI report is heavily dependent on the efficiency of the underlying in-memory database engine (VertiPaq). VertiPaq is a columnar database, meaning it compresses data column by column.

The compression ratio depends on the Cardinality of the column—the number of unique values. A column with a billion rows but only 2 unique values ('Yes'/'No') will compress down to almost nothing. However, a column with a billion unique timestamps (high cardinality) will consume massive amounts of RAM and severely degrade DAX query performance. Optimization requires eliminating unnecessary high-cardinality columns.
