Week 15: The Future of BI & Generative AI

Before we dive into our final class, test your knowledge on the modern trends of Business Intelligence. Select the best answer for each question.

1. What does AutoML stand for in the context of modern data platforms?
  • Correct! AutoML automates the process of applying machine learning models to real-world problems.
2. How does Generative AI (like Copilot) primarily assist a BI Analyst today?
  • Correct! AI assists by accelerating code writing and generating natural language insights, but still requires a solid data foundation.
3. According to modern BI trends, what will become the most valuable skill for a Data Analyst in the future?
