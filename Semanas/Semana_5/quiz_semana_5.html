English as a Medium of Instruction: KPIs and Performance Measurement

Analytical Context

A Key Performance Indicator (KPI) is not just a metric. A metric is a quantifiable measure (e.g., 'Total Views'), whereas a KPI is a strategic metric directly tied to a business objective with a defined target (e.g., 'Conversion Rate vs Target').

Effective KPIs follow the SMART framework: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. Furthermore, a dashboard should distinguish between Leading indicators (predictive, hard to measure) and Lagging indicators (historical, easy to measure).
