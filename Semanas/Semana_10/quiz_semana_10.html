English as a Medium of Instruction: Cloud Deployment & Automation

Analytical Context

Modern Business Intelligence requires automated delivery. Once a model is built locally, it is published to the Cloud (e.g., Power BI Service) into collaborative environments called Workspaces.

To ensure executives always have fresh data, architects configure a Scheduled Refresh via an On-Premises Data Gateway or direct cloud connection. The architecture fundamentally separates the Semantic Model (the database and DAX rules) from the Report (the visual interface), allowing multiple different reports to connect to a single, certified, and automated 'Golden Dataset'.
