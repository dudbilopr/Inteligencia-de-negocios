English as a Medium of Instruction: Executive Reporting & The SCR Framework

Analytical Context

The SCR (Situation, Complication, Resolution) framework, popularized by McKinsey, is the gold standard for structuring executive communications.

You begin by establishing the Situation (a stable, agreed-upon context). Then you introduce the Complication (the trigger, problem, or opportunity revealed by your data). Finally, you provide the Resolution (your actionable 'Big Idea' or recommendation). This structure hooks the audience immediately and drives decision-making.
