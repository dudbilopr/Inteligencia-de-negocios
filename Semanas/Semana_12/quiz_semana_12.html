English as a Medium of Instruction: Audience Adaptation

Analytical Context

When communicating analytical findings, knowing your audience is paramount. A C-Level executive (CEO, CFO) requires a macroscopic view focused on profitability and strategic decisions, often summarized in a few high-impact KPIs. They do not have time to explore complex data models or read through intricate DAX formulas.

Conversely, operational managers need granular, tactical data to optimize daily workflows. Presenting the wrong level of detail to the wrong audience will result in your analysis being ignored.
