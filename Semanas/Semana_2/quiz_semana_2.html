English as a Medium of Instruction: Dimensional Modeling & ETL

Analytical Context

In dimensional modeling, we separate data into Fact tables (containing quantitative business metrics) and Dimension tables (containing descriptive attributes). This is known as a Star Schema.

During the Extract, Transform, Load (ETL) process, analytical rigor dictates that we minimize transformations in the reporting layer and instead push them upstream to the data source or Power Query to optimize the VertiPaq engine's memory compression.
